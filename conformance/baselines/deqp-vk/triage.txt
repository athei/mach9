# Notes on the deqp-vk cases of the expected results in this directory, kept
# by hand.
#
# One line per pattern over case names (`*` matches any run of characters),
# then any of
#   issue=<n>  the mach9 issue that tracks the cases
#   flaky      their results change between runs of one build: the diff
#              reports their changes but fails only on a new crash or stall,
#              and `record` runs them three more times alone and writes the
#              worst result
# `#` starts a comment, on a line of its own or after the notes:
#
#   dEQP-VK.synchronization.basic.timeline_semaphore.* issue=63 flaky  # races the host
#
# A case that loses the device does not belong here: it harms the cases of
# other processes. Exclude its family in deqp-vk.toml with its issue instead.
